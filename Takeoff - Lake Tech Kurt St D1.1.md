### Honest notes on the measured numbers
- The flooring SF assumes the tagged rooms are rectangular and the tag sits inside the room — true for these four. **Two flooring areas in the detail-blow-up region couldn't be auto-bounded** and need a manual trace (they may be enlarged views duplicating rooms already counted — check before adding).
- Wall LF is each tag matched to its nearest wall segment (tags landed 0–3.6 ft from the matched wall, so the association is solid). My first automated pass mis-grabbed a 97-ft building wall for a D25 door-opening cut — corrected to ~3 ft each here.
- **Add base LF for D13** (resilient base runs the room perimeter) — roughly 2×(L+W) per room, ~190 LF across the three bounded resilient rooms.
